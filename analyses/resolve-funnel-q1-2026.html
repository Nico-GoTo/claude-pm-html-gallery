Executive Summary

This analysis examines the trial-to-paid conversion funnel for GoTo Resolve in Q1 2026. We identified three critical drop-off points that account for approximately 62% of all lost conversions.

Key Findings

The largest drop-off occurs between "device enrolled" and "first remote session," suggesting that users struggle to see immediate value from the product after initial setup.

Proposed Interventions

Three PLG-oriented interventions are proposed: an interactive onboarding wizard, a "first 5 minutes" guided experience, and proactive in-app nudges at day 2 and day 5 of the trial.

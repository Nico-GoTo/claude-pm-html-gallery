3

Review & Refine the Pitch Document

Claude produces a structured, data-backed pitch — review it and ask for any changes

📄
What the output looks like
Markdown · JIRA-ready
## The Idea
A native mobile app for GoTo Resolve, targeting SMB IT admins who need
to respond to device issues on the go...

## The Opportunity
Market: Mobile IT management growing at 18% CAGR (Gartner 2024).
Retention impact: SMB monthly churn currently at 4.2% — comparable
mobile features reduced churn by ~0.8pts in peer products...

## Competitive Landscape
NinjaRMM and Atera both ship mobile apps. GoTo Resolve currently has
no mobile offering — this is an explicit gap in Crayon battle cards...

## What Success Looks Like
— Reduce SMB churn from 4.2% → 3.5% within 12 months of launch
— 30% of SMB admins using mobile app within 6 months...
Refining the document
💬 Ask Claude to strengthen any section
📊 Request a different metric or angle
✂️ Ask it to shorten or expand
🔄 Swap in your own numbers
📋
When you're happy — copy the full output Select all text in Claude's response and copy it to your clipboard. It's already formatted for JIRA.
↓
4

Create the JIRA Item

Paste the pitch directly into a new JIRA initiative or epic

🎯
1
Go to JIRA and create a new Initiative or Epic item in the GoTo Resolve project
2
Give it a clear, short title that describes the idea (e.g. "Mobile App for GoTo Resolve — SMB Retention Play")
3
Paste the Claude-generated pitch document into the description field — JIRA renders the Markdown formatting automatically
4
Set the label or component to "Pitch" so it's easy to filter and track through the review process
Fields to fill in
📌 Item type: Initiative / Epic
🏷️ Label: Pitch
👤 Assignee: You
📋 Description: paste here
↓
5

Schedule in Opportunity Council

Your pitch goes in front of Resolve leadership for a go / no-go decision

🏛️
🏛️

Opportunity Council

Once your JIRA item is created, add it to the Opportunity Council meeting queue. This is a regular review with Resolve leadership where pitches are evaluated and decisions are made on what to pursue.

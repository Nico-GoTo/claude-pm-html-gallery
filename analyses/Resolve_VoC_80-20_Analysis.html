1. Remote Access & Session Reliability

Why This Is #1Critical

9. AI Features — Strategic Context

Internal Assessment (from your own Slack)

Your own analysis from January 2026 captures the state clearly:

"Since joining Resolve again and looking at the AI features and usage/adoption, I have very little faith in this being something that people have a willingness to pay for in its current state."

Key data points from customer interviews and internal analysis:

  • Session Notes — Most "used" feature but auto-generated, so true willingness-to-pay is unknown. Wolters Kluwer (38K ARR) gave strong positive feedback: saves up to 1hr/day on KB article creation.
  • Repeat usage is low — Both adoption volume and repeat usage suggest insufficient value for most users.
  • Resolution Agent / One-Click Resolution — Was planned for March closed beta; status uncertain. Sales is asking about it.
  • Your proposed framework: Monetize only action-oriented "Category 3" features (device executions, automations); keep everything else as free substrate to build habit and trust.

For the 80/20 analysis: AI features are not currently a VoC pain point — customers aren't complaining about AI. The risk is in premature monetization creating friction before value is proven. The opportunity is in Resolution Agent and proactive diagnostics, which directly tie to the Nexus thesis.

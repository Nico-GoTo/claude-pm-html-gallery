The Idea

We add a lightweight, deterministic endpoint health scoring engine to GoTo Resolve that continuously evaluates endpoints across performance and security dimensions — and surfaces actionable findings to IT technicians before users file tickets. Unlike enterprise-grade DEX platforms such as Nexthink, this is designed to be fast to deploy, easy to interpret, and built for the SMB and mid-market technicians who already use Resolve. Think of it as a proactive assistant that spots the fire before the smoke alarm goes off.

The Problem

Today, GoTo Resolve is fundamentally a reactive tool. Technicians respond to tickets, join remote sessions, and fix issues after users are already impacted. This is the exact mode our board has flagged as a strategic liability.

  • Technicians spend the majority of their time responding to issues rather than preventing them — the ticket queue never empties.
  • End users experience productivity loss before IT is even aware. A machine at 95% memory for three days doesn't generate a ticket — it generates a frustrated user who eventually can't work.
  • Security gaps — missing patches, disabled antivirus, unencrypted drives — sit undetected until an incident occurs or an audit fails.
  • This is a long-standing customer ask and a direct board request to shift our product posture from reactive to proactive. We have heard this consistently across segments, but have yet to ship a native answer to it.

Proposed Approach
Phase 1 · MVP

Endpoint Health Score + Technician Findings Feed

  • Deterministic scoring model across performance health (CPU, memory, disk, battery) and security posture (patch status, AV state, encryption, firewall)
  • Composite health score (0–100) per endpoint, recalculated on a configurable cadence
  • Dedicated Proactive Findings view in the Resolve dashboard — filterable by severity, device, site, or category
  • Technicians can review, dismiss, or immediately launch a remote session or automated remediation from the finding card
  • No AI black box — every score is explainable with a clear breakdown of contributing factors
Phase 2 · If Validated

Automated Remediations + Trend Alerting

  • Pre-built remediation scripts triggered automatically on high-confidence findings (e.g. start AV scan, clear temp files, push a missing patch)
  • Trend tracking per endpoint: "this device has been flagged for low disk space 4 times in 30 days"
  • Aggregate fleet health view for IT managers
  • Configurable threshold management for MSPs and power users

What We Need to Decide

Pricing & Packaging

Is this a premium add-on, a tier differentiator, or included in existing RMM plans? This decision drives the build vs. monetization tradeoff before we scope engineering.

Scoring Model Ownership

Do we define and maintain the health scoring rules internally, or allow customers/partners to configure thresholds? The latter adds complexity but significantly increases stickiness.

Agent Requirements

Does proactive diagnostics require additional agent capabilities, or can we build on the existing RMM agent footprint? Engineering input needed before final scoping.

MSP vs. Internal IT First

Should MVP target MSP technicians managing many endpoints, or internal IT teams managing a single org? The workflow and UX differ meaningfully — getting this wrong affects adoption.

Board Framing

Given this is a direct board ask, how do we frame the update — as a committed roadmap item, or as a pitch seeking Phase 1 investment approval?
